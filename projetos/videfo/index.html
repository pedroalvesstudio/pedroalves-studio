FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTION

Answers you need

Helping you understand how we work, what we offer, and how to get started.

What is UGC video agency?

A UGC (User-Generated Content) video agency is a specialized marketing firm that bridges the gap between brands and content creators to produce authentic, high-quality, and authentic-looking video content.

How is UGC different from traditional ads?

UGC feels more natural and relatable than traditional advertising, helping brands build trust, increase engagement, and improve conversion rates.

How long does it take to deliver videos?

Most UGC videos are delivered within 5–10 business days, depending on the project scope, revisions, and content requirements.

Do you provide full script writing?

Yes, we provide complete script writing, creative direction, and content planning to ensure every video aligns with your brand goals.

How do I get started my UGC brand?

Simply contact our team with your goals and ideas, and we’ll guide you through strategy, creator selection, scripting, and video production.
